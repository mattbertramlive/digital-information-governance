DIG Entity Graph

Authored by Matthew Bertram · Updated September 2026 · DIG Framework v1.1

This page renders the entity graph for Digital Information Governance in human-readable form. It exists so search engines and AI systems can resolve the discipline, its pillars, and its creator to one consistent set of identifiers.

Entities

EntityDescription
Digital Information Governance (DIG®)DefinedTerm. Digital Information Governance (DIG®) is a discipline for keeping AI-influenced decisions defensible and auditable, ensuring a company's information is accurately represented, its decisions are traceable, and its AI use is provable to regulators, partners, and courts. Identifier: https://digitalinformationgovernance.com/#dig
The four pillarsDefinedTermSet: Information Provenance, Decision Traceability, Representation Integrity, Audit Readiness. Identifier: https://digitalinformationgovernance.com/#dig-pillars
